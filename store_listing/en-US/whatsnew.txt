The app now has a reader: upload your own books and read with tap-to-translate.

• EPUB, FB2, PDF, TXT and pasted text are all supported
• Tap any word for its translation and meaning right in that sentence, no dictionary lookup needed
• A word saved from a book gets the sense that actually fits that sentence
• Bookmarks, table of contents and your reading position are remembered automatically
• PDF files open directly in the app
